Drugs by Places, the preface

De compositione medicamentorum secundum locos I.1 · Kühn XII 378–381

Galen on why a recipe book starts at the top of the head — the scalp as a plowed field of Demeter's grain — with the translator's working published beside the text. No prior published English rendering is known.
